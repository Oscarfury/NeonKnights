# Castle defense direction — 5 September 2026

The user's latest direction supersedes the earlier free-roaming commander design for the main game:

- A fixed castle is the central defense objective, with the King fighting on its battlements.
- Rotational controls move the King around the battlements to cover threatened sides. The castle itself stays fixed.
- The existing knight models become a persistent, recruitable and equippable company. They deploy through the gates and fight automatically; they are not temporary summons.
- Purchased defenses occupy the wall bastions. The castle has three modeled wall tiers with real upgrades to its silhouette, battlements and structure.
- The King is the legendary unit. King powers, equipment choices, wall investment and company composition supply player decisions alongside rotation.
- The courtyard remains a separate art and combat testing area. Its free movement controls do not define the new castle game.

The existing asset-quality requirements, physical boss attacks, separate progression screens, meaningful legendary effects and late progression requirements still apply. The first integrated siege should demonstrate castle rotation controls, automatic company combat, wall-mounted construction, a real boss and planning decisions together.
