# Fifteen-level campaign implementation

6 September 2026. This release corrects the previous delivery: the expansion had been committed as design documents while the executable still stopped after three watches.

## Playable content

- Fifteen authored encounters use `src/next/castle/campaign-data.json`, with fixed bounties, completion rewards, front limits, live enemy caps and short approach cues. The council shows every level and the current act. No boss appears on level two.
- Eight ordinary roles: Raider, Bulwark, Ash Archer, Sapper, Hexcaster, Banneret, Iron Ram and Reaver. Roles approach reserved wall positions, commit facing, and resume their assignment after a bounded diversion. Rams reserve additional space, sappers can be killed to cancel their fuse, ranged troops fire behind their screen, and reavers pursue vulnerable ranged knights.
- The Golem, Emberwing and Hollow King occupy levels 5, 10 and 15. Golem stone attacks, Dragon fire, and Hollow King decrees share their rendered footprint with collision. The Hollow King moves between sectors as wounded and plants a destructible escort standard. A broken Golem throwing arm removes its boulder attack. Major wall warnings share one reservation, and a timed Guard exposes bosses.
- Four unique attached castle wings: Tavern, Sanctuary, Forge and War Room. Every wing has three visual tiers and a combat purpose. Four outer mount families include the new physical-shell Mortar. Every defense has two selectable rank-III paths.
- Six recruits across four classes, three active positions, promotion gates, two talent branches per class, one talent point per rank and full between-level healing. Lancers charge; Chanters heal and cast light bolts. New equipment attaches to the shared animated humanoid rig.
- Stormbow and Sunlance each have two weapon paths. Six royal runes and eight knight items enter the shop through progression gates. Six relics support two owned slots and one ascension, earned after levels 5, 10 and 13. Secondary effects do not recursively trigger royal shot procs.

## Tuning and scope

The design worksheets remain historical starting values. Combat testing changed boss health to **3,800 / 7,600 / 11,000**, and ordinary enemy health to **1 / 1.4 / 1.9** across the acts. Empty fronts may advance a packet up to three seconds early. Ash Archer uses a bow model and bow motion, rather than claiming an unauthored crossbow asset. Elites are stronger named variants of existing roles. The class talent registry contains the shipped descriptions; several talent names and values differ from the original worksheet.

Normal and Veteran are available. This remains a deterministic arcade campaign, with no endless mode in the castle client. Balance checks are automated; human difficulty and low-end mobile performance still need broader playtesting.

## Persistence and URLs

The main URL and `/next/` launch the same client. The previous Phaser game is archived at `/v2/`. Campaign version 2 uses `neon-knights:v3:campaign:2`; earlier three-watch saves are kept separately. Fifteen-level completion reloads into the victory council rather than wrapping into a missing encounter.

## Verification

**130 tests pass**, all **29 GLBs validate**, and the production build passes. A legal simulation clears all 15 levels. Browser inputs clear levels 1, 5, 8, 10 and 15; completed saves reload correctly. A 412×915 production browser verifies Tavern construction, recruitment, Guard, pause, a newly built War Room, checkpoint reload and no horizontal overflow. The eight-building desktop fixture measures 16.7 ms median / 16.8 ms p95 over 180 frames.

The release checks include simulation, economy, talent ownership, gates, late saves, fuse cancellation, physical mortar impacts and new asset contracts. `tools/review/play-campaign.ts` plays the entire campaign with legal transactions and normal inputs. `tools/review/check-campaign-browser.js` exercises opening, mid-campaign and all three boss encounters through mouse and keyboard input, saving and reloading each victory. The browser review uses checkpoints generated by the legal simulation to select those encounters.

The authored Golem has a 13-joint skeleton, nine animation clips, textured materials and a separate breakable arm. Mortars retain their operating pivots; the Ram has a moving battering head. New rigid details are batched by material within their pivots. Existing body batching, cached scenery shadows and bounded render resolution remain enabled.

Current reports are collected in `evidence/campaign-expansion.json`. Full screenshots and intermediate captures are local under `output/playwright/`.
